Who We Are

About KBS

38+

Years of
Heritage

A Legacy Built on Passion & Tradition

Since 11 May 1988, Perkhidmatan Dan Jualan Kanesan Bersaudara (KBS) has been a trusted name in the community, based in Simpang, Taiping, Perak.

As an Official Agent of Syarikat Kilang Rempa Jaya Sakti Sdn. Bhd., we distribute premium spice blends and curry powders — carefully sourced and crafted using time-honoured recipes.

Beyond the kitchen, KBS is also a trusted supplier of authentic Indian prayer items — serving the spiritual needs of our community with the same dedication we bring to our spices.
